## Design Context

Users: Velcure customers in medium-sized businesses, HSE, HR and occupational physicians. They need clear, reliable access to their work.

Brand: mutig, direkt, eigenständig; German Du-Ansprache. Use the approved Nah + Fokus identity. Deep petrol #032f2e, restrained mint #dcefe5, canvas #f2f6f3. Archivo headings, Geist reading text. Radii 8/12/20/28px. One Medical was the founder’s preferred reference for a human health-oriented identity.

Principles: Authentication comes first. No marketing carousel, invented trust claims or decorative animation. Clear labels, keyboard operation, readable errors and sufficient contrast. Preserve Keycloak’s native security and interaction behaviour. Email uses system sans-serif fallbacks and PNG logos for client compatibility.

Sources: velcure/lp release v0.6.0, `packages/brand` and `packages/tokens/src/tokens.json`. Assets are a versioned snapshot, not a runtime dependency on the marketing app.
